Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about AgroVision? We have answers.

How does AgroVision map scheme eligibility?
AgroVision matches your profile metrics (land holding size, irrigation status, state location, and crops grown) directly against a digital rules index covering central initiatives (e.g. PM-KISAN, PM-KMY) and state-specific credit schemes.
Is my certificate upload vault secure?
Yes, all uploaded records, identity credentials, and land certificates are processed locally inside our Document AI system and stored in your private, secure credentials vault.
Which regional Indian languages are supported?
The platform currently offers translation settings for English, Hindi, Marathi, and Telugu. All video tutorials, buttons, and chatbot advisories support local voice input matching.
